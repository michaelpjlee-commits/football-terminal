> MATCH.REPORT · DURÁN DELIVERS

Camilo Durán struck twice, including an 87th-minute winner, as Celtic came from behind to beat Beşiktaş 3-2 at Celtic Park. All five goals arrived after a goalless first half.

Sam Johnstone saved Junior Olaïtan’s penalty in the 54th minute, but Olaïtan scored moments later from close range. Celtic’s triple change at 61 minutes transformed the contest: Callum Osmand levelled from outside the box a minute later, then supplied Durán for the 78th-minute lead.

Orkun Kökçü equalised from distance in the 85th minute. Durán answered two minutes later to restore Celtic’s advantage. Beşiktaş had more possession and attempts, but Celtic were more accurate in front of goal.
