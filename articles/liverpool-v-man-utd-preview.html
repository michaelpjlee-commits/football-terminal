Liverpool vs Manchester United: Anfield preview

Sunday 22 November 2026, 4.30pm UK, Anfield

Liverpool host Manchester United at Anfield at 4.30pm UK time on Sunday 22 November, the first league weekend back after the international break, live on Sky Sports. Liverpool sit fourth, with 18 points from 10 games. United are 18th, with eight points and a new head coach. Mauricio Pochettino's first match in charge is this one. For Liverpool fans, the question is whether Anfield can start feeling like Anfield again. For United fans, it is whether a new voice can halt a slide that has left the club in the relegation zone.

Recent form

On paper the gap is wide. Look a little closer, and both sets of supporters have reasons to feel uneasy.

Liverpool have taken 12 points from their last six league matches, including a 3-1 win at Crystal Palace on 8 November, with goals from Alexis Mac Allister, Florian Wirtz and Alexander Isak. They also beat Bournemouth 1-0 away, Brentford 2-1 away and Brighton 2-0 at home. The two defeats in that run both came at Anfield: 0-3 to Manchester City and 0-1 to Arsenal.

The home record will unsettle Liverpool supporters. In five league games at Anfield this season, Andoni Iraola's side have one win, two draws and two defeats, ranking 18th in the home table. Away from home they are a different team: four wins and a draw, first in the away table.

United have one league win all season, the 5-2 defeat of Ipswich on 30 August. Their last six league games have produced only four points: draws with Fulham, Tottenham and Leeds, a 0-3 home defeat to Bournemouth, a 0-2 loss at Chelsea, and a 4-4 draw with Aston Villa on 8 November. They have not won away in the league and have yet to keep a clean sheet. Liverpool have four. Both clubs have scored 14 league goals; Liverpool have conceded 10, United 19.

Head to head

In 52 Premier League meetings since 2000-01, United lead 23-18, with 11 draws. Liverpool have scored more goals in that span, 74 to 62, but United have taken more wins.

Recent history also favours the visitors. United have won the last two league meetings: 2-1 at Anfield in October 2025, and 3-2 at Old Trafford on 3 May 2026. Liverpool's last league win in the fixture was 3-0 at Old Trafford on 1 September 2024. The 7-0 at Anfield in March 2023 remains the most famous recent result, but that is now more than three years ago.

Key players

Alexander Isak is the clear threat for Liverpool, with seven Premier League goals in 10 games. Dominik Szoboszlai's fitness is a concern after the international break. Virgil van Dijk is expected to be available after sitting out Netherlands duty with a minor niggle.

For United, Bruno Fernandes and Bryan Mbeumo have four league goals each and are the main attacking outlet, especially with Benjamin Sesko ruled out until the new year. Marcus Rashford is a possible option if he comes through a hip problem. Lisandro Martinez is back from suspension and should be available.

Team news

As of 15 November 2026, unless a later date is given. Several players are still on international duty, so this can still change before Sunday.

Liverpool — Out: Hugo Ekitike (long-term lower-leg problem, not expected before January); Giovanni Leoni (knee, return already delayed, not expected before January); Conor Bradley (knee); Jeremie Frimpong (thigh — a 22 November return date has been listed, but he was still ruled out on 15 November). Doubt: Ryan Gravenberch (heel, day to day); Dominik Szoboszlai (knee). Szoboszlai came off in the win at Palace, then played 90 minutes for Hungary in Georgia on 14 November, later admitting he almost withdrew in the warm-up and needed more treatment before Hungary's game against Northern Ireland on Tuesday 17 November. Expected to be available: Virgil van Dijk, who withdrew from the Netherlands squad after Palace with a niggle but was not listed as injured on 15 November.

Manchester United — Out: Manuel Ugarte (knee ligament injury from the summer, not expected back until 2027); Benjamin Sesko (shin, not expected before January); Carlos Baleba (hamstring, a number of weeks and not during this break). Doubt: Luke Shaw (muscular problem, hoped-for return 22 November); Marcus Rashford (hip, assessed day by day); Harry Maguire (back spasm, withdrawn at half-time against Villa); Patrick Dorgu, sent home from Denmark duty on 13 November after failing fitness tests, with reports on 14 November that he could miss Anfield. Available: Lisandro Martinez, whose suspension for two yellow cards has been served.

Pochettino's first match

Mauricio Pochettino was confirmed as Manchester United head coach on 13 November, on a contract until the summer of 2029. This trip to Anfield will be his first game in charge.

The 54-year-old Argentine has managed Tottenham, Paris Saint-Germain, Chelsea and the United States. At Tottenham he built a side that reached the 2019 Champions League final. This summer he took the USA to the last 16 of the World Cup. United's own statement pointed to his high-intensity, attacking style and his record of developing players.

He does not yet have a United record to judge. He was due to finish the USA's Nations League ties with Haiti before travelling to Manchester, with only a few training days before Sunday. That limits how much can change in one afternoon. What both sets of supporters will look for is a higher press, more intensity, and a clearer idea of how the team wants to play.

For Liverpool, the task is to make home form match the standard they have set on the road. For United, even a point on the new coach's first day would feel like a start.

Facts worth knowing

  1. Liverpool are first in the Premier League away table and 18th in the home table after five games in each.
  2. United's only league win this season is 5-2 against Ipswich on 30 August. They have not kept a clean sheet in 10 league games.
  3. United have won the last two league meetings, including a 2-1 victory at Anfield in October 2025.
  4. Both teams have scored 14 league goals; United have conceded almost twice as many as Liverpool.
